# Helping a Friend Troubleshoot a GPU Crash

What I did: I helped a friend figure out why their NVIDIA RTX 5070 kept crashing in Marvel Rivals on Windows. We traced it to a driver-level hang in Unreal Engine. running multiple terminal commands to see where the crash was beginning and what was causing it narrowing it down to be a heat issue.

When: July/2026.

What was good:I enjoyed the brainstorming with my friend bouncing  back and forth ideas trying different commands to see when the crash was happening.

Favorite memory: when we finally were able to figure it was the heat because it was then easy to fix

What I didn't enjoy: The multiple times the computer just froze and we were unable to do anything because of a crash.

Grade: A
